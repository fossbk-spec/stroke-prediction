# RANG BUOC CONG BO AI CHO TAC GIA

1. AI KHONG DUOC DUNG TEN DONG TAC GIA: cong cu AI khong chiu trach nhiem phap ly
   va dao duc cho bai bao - dung mot minh con nguoi dung ten.
2. BAT BUOC KHAI BAO MINH BACH: neu co dung AI ho tro viet/soan thao/sua van phong,
   khai bao ro ten cong cu va muc dich su dung, dat dung vi tri tap chi yeu cau
   (xem .agents/skills/viet-bai-bao-khoa-hoc/references/02-tuan-thu-cong-bo-ai-khi-viet.md).
3. TAC GIA CHIU TRACH NHIEM 100%: cong thuc toan hoc, so lieu thuc nghiem, tinh
   chinh xac khoa hoc va danh muc trich dan thuoc trach nhiem cua dong tac gia
   con nguoi, khong duoc do loi cho AI.
4. ZERO-HALLUCINATION: moi citekey [@...] trong ban thao phai ton tai that trong
   references/library.bib, khong duoc AI tu bia tai lieu hoac suy dien nam xuat ban.
5. TU SOI BAI TRUOC KHI NOP: nap journal_profiles/<nhom>/<target_journal>.json
   truoc khi hoan tat ban thao, tu dong vai phan bien doc lap cho chinh minh
   (xem .agents/skills/viet-bai-bao-khoa-hoc/references/03-tu-kiem-tra-truoc-khi-nop.md).
